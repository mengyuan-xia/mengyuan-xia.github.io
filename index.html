Welcome! I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Economics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK). I received my Ph.D. in Economics from CUHK in 2026.

My research interests are in Industrial Organization, Applied Microeconomics, and Environmental Economics. I use reduced-form empirical methods and structural estimation to study consumer behavior, market outcomes, and environmental issues. My earlier work in theoretical industrial organization includes a publication in Journal of Economics & Management Strategy.

I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market.
